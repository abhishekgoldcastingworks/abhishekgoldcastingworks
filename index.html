About The Workshop

Precision Is Our Craft.

Abhishek Gold Casting Works specializes in gold casting, jewellery moulding and detailed jewellery manufacturing work in Karimnagar, Telangana. Every piece that leaves our workshop passes through careful hands and a disciplined process — from mould preparation to the final polish.

We work closely with jewellery shop owners, manufacturers and designers, translating ideas and CAD models into precisely cast gold forms ready for finishing.

How We Work

From Mould To Masterpiece

01

Design

The jewellery concept or client reference is reviewed and prepared for moulding.

02

Moulding

A precise mould is prepared to capture every detail of the design.

03

Model Preparation

Wax or model trees are prepared and readied for the casting stage.

04

Gold Casting

Gold is cast into the prepared mould under controlled conditions.

05

Finishing

Cast pieces are cleaned, refined and prepared for the next stage of jewellery making.

06

Quality Check

Every casting is checked for accuracy, consistency and finish before dispatch.
